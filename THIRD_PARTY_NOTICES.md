## Endless question bank

`data/endless-bank.jsonl.gz` (10,000 prompts) was generated by `scripts/endless/generate.py` from open data. Its rarity tiers are an automatic proxy: answers inside each prompt are ranked by how common the word or phrase is (or by population, for cities), not by player answers. Because the tiers are derived from wordfreq data, the bank file as a whole is offered under **CC BY-SA 4.0**, separately from the MIT application code. Sources:

- WordNet 3.1, Princeton University (WordNet License, permissive; https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use) — categories, multi-word terms and the English dictionary.
- wordfreq by Robyn Speer (data CC BY-SA 4.0) — word and phrase frequencies used to rank answers.
- GeoNames via geonamescache (CC BY 4.0, https://www.geonames.org) — countries, capitals, neighbours, currencies and cities with populations.
- CMU Pronouncing Dictionary via pronouncing (BSD-style) — rhymes.
- ISO 639 language names via pycountry (iso-codes data).
- List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words via naughty-words (CC BY 4.0) — used only to filter content out.
